# MindHause Free Asset Findings

## Quaternius (CC0)
- **Fantasy Props MegaKit**: 200+ stylized props. Perfect for Victorian (books, candles) and Greco-Roman (vases, statues).
- **Medieval Village MegaKit**: 300+ models. Useful for Victorian and Countryside Cottage architecture/props.
- **Sci-Fi Essentials Kit**: Guns/props, but useful for Sci-Fi Minimal theme.
- **Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit**: Environment pieces for Sci-Fi Minimal.
- **Stylized Nature MegaKit**: Ghibli-inspired, perfect for Japanese Ryokan and Countryside Cottage.
- **Ultimate Furniture Pack**: High-quality furniture for Modern Luxury Loft and Victorian themes.
- **Ultimate Buildings Pack**: Architecture foundations.
- **Modular Dungeon Pack**: Arches and pillars for Gothic Cathedral or Greco-Roman.
- **Sushi Restaurant Kit**: Props for Japanese Ryokan.
