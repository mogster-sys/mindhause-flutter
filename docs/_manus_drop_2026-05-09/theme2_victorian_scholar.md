# Theme 2: Victorian Scholar - Ecosystem Curation

## Best Foundation Environment Packs
- **Victorian (KitBash3D)**:
  - **URL**: https://kitbash3d.com/products/victorian

  - **License**: KitBash3D Individual/Small Business
  - **Value**: Includes University Headquarters, Library of Parliament, and Nobleman Mansion. Essential for the "scholarly warmth" and "intellectual refuge" foundations.
  - **Style Compatibility**: 5/5.
  - **Godot/Mobile Suitability**: 3/5 (Requires optimization).

- **POLYGON - Fantasy Dungeon (Synty Studios)**:
